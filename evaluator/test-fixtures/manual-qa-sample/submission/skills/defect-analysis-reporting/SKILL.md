# Defect Analysis and Reporting

## Process

1. Review the evidence (screenshots, logs) supplied by the user.
2. Identify error patterns and root-cause hypotheses.
3. Separate facts from assumptions and hypotheses.
4. Assess impact, severity and priority (guide: `references/severity-guide.md`).
5. Export the bug list by copying `assets/bug-template.xlsx` and filling the sheet "Bug List".

## Notes

- Open `references/severity-guide.md` at Step 4.
- Open `assets/bug-template.xlsx` at Step 5. Keep the dropdowns.
- Environment fields (build, browser, OS) are filled only if the exam provides them; otherwise leave empty and write "not provided" in Note.
